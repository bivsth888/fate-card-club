Player balances

Play together on one screen

  1. Tap Shuffle & deal. Everyone gets two face-down cards.
  2. The highlighted player’s pair flips face-up automatically.
  3. Choose a bet up to the pot, or pass for free. Then tap Draw third card.
  4. Strictly between the two ranks: collect your bet. Outside: pay your bet. Match either rank: pay your bet, or double when “Match pays 2×” is on. Change the switch at any time; completed results stay unchanged.
  5. Pairs and adjacent ranks have no winning gap. Take the free pass.
  6. After the third card flips, you have 0.8 seconds to see it before the result animation. The next turn starts automatically. A new round shuffles a fresh 52-card deck after everyone has played.
